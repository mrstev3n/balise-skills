# Asset strategy

Use this reference to decide whether to reuse, fabricate, source, generate, or render the visual matter of a motion-graphics sequence.

Load [asset-acquisition.md](asset-acquisition.md) for the portable still-image workflow, live Magnific/Higgsfield routing, local dependency setup, chroma key, transparency, overscan, and compositing checks.

## Priority order

1. Audit the complete official identity and product assets.
2. Reuse or adapt official editable assets when they serve the concept.
3. Fabricate coherent SVG, type, Canvas, or code-driven assets when the system needs control.
4. Source or generate only where a real gap remains.
5. Use 3D, shaders, or Three.js only when space, light, material, or camera carries the idea.

Do not interpret this order rigidly. A generated environment can be the concept. The rule is to avoid replacing an available identity with approximate decoration by accident.

Never regenerate, “improve,” complete, or reinterpret an official logo with an image model. Obtain a better source or reconstruct it faithfully as vector geometry, then require explicit validation before animation.

## Route matrix

| Route | Good fit | Required proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official asset | Existing identity or real product | Source, correct version, proportions, color, type, rights |
| Generated raster | Transparent subject, texture, object, collage, environment | Brief, sample in hero frame, resolution, alpha/edges, series consistency |
| Licensed stock | Credible footage, sound, texture, 3D, vector, Lottie | Correct asset, usage terms, required attribution, acquisition record |
| SVG or Canvas | Recolorable, crisp, parametric shapes or masks | Final-size render, DOM/complexity check, stable morphing |
| Three.js or shader | Narrative depth, camera, light, material, spatial transition | Determinism, performance, fallback, comparison with simpler route |
| Flat composition | Type, rhythm, color, and layout are intentionally sufficient | Reference-backed art direction, strong hero frame |

## Proactive decision process

1. Mark which scenes lack subject, matter, depth, scale, or continuity.
2. Inspect the harness for relevant native image, Magnific, Higgsfield, video, SVG, 3D, shader, stock, and sound capabilities.
3. In guided mode, present one to three routes with benefit, effort, risk, and minimum validation. In autonomous or hybrid mode, choose the strongest reversible route and make one sample.
4. In authorized production, create or acquire one sample only.
5. Place it in a representative hero frame.
6. Validate the sample before series production.

Do not ask the user to know the tool name. Do not claim a capability exists until it is exposed in the current harness. If a local still-image backend is missing, use the packaged setup when installation is authorized. If tools, installation, and network are unavailable, prepare a precise generation or sourcing brief and label the result unverified.

## Generation brief

Specify:

- narrative role and exact scene placement;
- silhouette, crop, perspective, camera or focal feeling;
- light direction, contrast, material, palette, and visual era;
- negative space needed for typography or movement;
- alpha or background requirement, output ratio, dimensions, margins, and variants;
- relationship with existing identity assets;
- negative constraints: no invented text, altered logos, unwanted background, pseudo-tech filler, stock gradient, or unrelated decoration.

Prefer the best live route rather than a permanent provider ranking. A request for transparency does not prove that a generator will deliver clean native alpha. Prefer a verified provider cutout, then use the packaged chroma workflow for simple opaque subjects. Route complex hair, fur, glass, smoke, liquids, reflections, translucency, and soft shadows to specialist matting.

## Acceptance checks

- The asset improves a named narrative, compositional, or motion problem.
- The visual style remains coherent across a series.
- Resolution and crop survive the final output dimensions and camera moves.
- Transparent assets have clean edges on light and dark test backgrounds.
- Generated text, logos, hands, faces, or product details are inspected closely.
- Looped video backgrounds are checked across the seam.
- 3D and shaders remain deterministic in the chosen render path.
- Every external asset actually integrated has the usage terms needed for delivery.

Reject mass generation before a representative sample is approved. Reject visual richness that only increases effect count.
