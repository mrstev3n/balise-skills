# Worked example — 12-second launch teaser

Use this example to understand the expected level of specificity. Do not reuse its duration, visual language, or timing values as defaults.

## Brief extract

- Purpose: announce that a collaborative research product is opening early access.
- Audience: design and research teams seeing the brand for the first time.
- Channel: silent-first vertical social post with an optional sound version.
- Essential message: scattered fragments become one usable shared body of knowledge.
- Working assumption: 12-second `9:16` master, 30 fps, one derived `1:1` cut.

## Direction

**Thesis:** fragments behave like magnetized paper fibres that align into a calm, legible system.

- Visual system: warm paper field, dark typographic fragments, one saturated identity color at resolution.
- Signature transition: dispersed fragments accelerate on curved paths, occlude one another, then settle into the product name.
- Asset route: official wordmark plus code-generated paper fragments and one generated texture sample.
- Avoid: fake interface, counters, generic particles, glass cards, unexplained 3D.

## Motion DNA

- Timing palette: `snap` 4–6 frames; `move` 12–18 frames; `resolve` 24–36 frames; holds driven by reading.
- Forces: quick exponential exits for fragments, soft damped settle for the final lockup.
- Amplitude: large spatial travel only for the signature convergence; secondary copy uses short masked movement.
- Density: one high-density convergence, otherwise sparse.
- Depth: fragment occlusion and scale establish three planes; no camera orbit.
- Typography: line/word masks, stable readable regime, final lockup held long enough for two reads.
- Sound: optional paper impacts and one low tonal resolution; silent version remains complete.

## Beat sheet extract

| Time | Scene job | Message | Visual action | Transition | Sound cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–2.5 s | Hook | Knowledge begins fragmented | Paper fragments enter from different depths; two short words become briefly readable | Hard editorial interruption at peak density | Dry paper ticks |
| 2.5–7.5 s | Transformation | Fragments become a shared system | Curved convergence with overlap; fragments form rows and reveal the promise | Continuous causal motion, no intermediate dead pose | Rising texture and impacts |
| 7.5–12.0 s | Resolution | Product name + early access | Rows compress into the official wordmark; CTA appears by line mask; final state holds | Soft settle and clean finite ending | Tonal resolve |

## Proof gates

- Direction: thesis and avoid list make the product claim accurate.
- Composition: first frame, density peak, and final lockup approved as hero frames.
- Rhythm: complete 12-second animatic readable without sound.
- Craft: convergence transition rendered at final quality and checked with dense frames.
- Final: actual export decoded, inspected, and tested in the target feed crop.
