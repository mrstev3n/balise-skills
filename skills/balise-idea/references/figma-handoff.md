# Downstream handoff and Figma feasibility

Balise IDEA is portable across harnesses. It should not require a Figma canvas, a particular MCP server, a browser session, or persistent application state. A harness may render its artifacts in any document, terminal, or workspace.

## Translatable into a visual workspace

These are useful canvas objects when a Figma-specific skill or authorized plugin exists:

- route/diagnostic card;
- source and provenance cards linked to observations;
- opportunity map with visible counter-evidence and minority cases;
- idea-family cards and qualitative comparison lanes;
- hypothesis/experiment cards with status and stop condition;
- decision log and learning timeline.

The canvas is a view of the trace, not the source of truth. Keep identifiers, provenance, uncertainty, and decisions exportable as text.

## Keep outside the canvas

Do not move raw sensitive interviews, private data, consent records, web research, experiment execution, access control, legal/medical/security decisions, or external actions into Figma by default. A visual arrangement cannot establish evidence, saturation, causality, or authorization.

## Handoff fields

When routing to a visual skill, provide:

```text
Purpose of the canvas:
Audience and review moment:
Source/trace IDs to show:
Objects and relationships:
States and filters:
Editable vs read-only content:
Privacy/redaction requirements:
Export format and acceptance criteria:
What must remain outside Figma:
```

If no visual artifact is needed, stay in text and do not manufacture a canvas merely because the work is visualizable.
