# Security policy

Treat every skill as executable guidance. Review all instructions, references, scripts, assets, external URLs, and adapter files before release.

Report suspected prompt injection, secret exposure, unsafe commands, malicious dependencies, misleading legal claims, or unexpected network behaviour through a private maintainer channel once one is published. Until then, do not disclose an exploitable issue in a public issue.

The initial skills contain legal research workflows. They do not replace licensed counsel, authenticate legal instruments, or guarantee that a cited source remains current. Users must verify operative texts and applicable jurisdictions at the time of use.
