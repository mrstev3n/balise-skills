# Balise IDEA — visual workspace edition

Use this edition to build a visual view of a text-based decision and evidence trace. The canvas supports discussion and review; it does not establish evidence, authorization, saturation, causality, or runtime behavior.

## Canvas role

Treat the canvas as a view of an exportable trace. Keep identifiers, provenance, uncertainty, and decisions readable and recoverable outside the canvas.

Useful objects include:

- route and diagnostic cards;
- source and provenance cards linked to observations;
- an opportunity map with counter-evidence and minority cases;
- idea-family cards and qualitative comparison lanes;
- hypothesis and experiment cards with status and stop condition;
- a decision log and learning timeline.

## Required fields

Every linked object should retain, where relevant:

- a stable ID;
- source or provenance information;
- the current uncertainty or evidence limit;
- the decision, owner, or next reversible action;
- status and stop or escalation conditions.

## Keep outside the canvas

Keep raw or sensitive corpus material, consent records, web research, experiment execution, access-control decisions, legal, medical, or security decisions, and external actions outside the canvas by default. A visual arrangement cannot establish evidence, authorization, causality, or safety.

## Construction procedure

1. State the purpose of the canvas, audience, and review moment.
2. Add only the route, sources, observations, opportunities, options, hypotheses, experiments, and decisions needed for that review.
3. Link each claim or object to its source ID and mark uncertainty, counter-evidence, minority cases, and missing information.
4. Mark editable and read-only content, privacy or redaction requirements, and any fields that must remain outside the canvas.
5. Check that the visual arrangement does not imply validation, completion, authorization, or a decision that the text trace does not support.

## Export and acceptance contract

Export a text-readable record that preserves IDs, source links, object relationships, status, uncertainty, decisions, owners, next reversible actions, and stop or escalation conditions.

Accept the canvas only when the export is understandable without the visual layout, the source trace remains the authority, sensitive material stays outside the canvas, and no visual grouping is presented as proof of evidence, causality, saturation, authorization, or runtime behavior.
